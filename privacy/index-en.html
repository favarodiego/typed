Privacy Policy - TypeD

Last updated: November 26, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to TypeD ("we", "our" or "app"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our social network application for people with diabetes.

By using TypeD, you agree to the practices described in this policy. We recommend that you read this document carefully.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Registration Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Bio (optional)

2.2 Health Information

We collect health information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Type of diabetes (Type 1, Type 2, Gestational, etc.)
  • Time since diagnosis
  • Type of treatment used
  • Glucose monitoring frequency
  • Glycemic control
  • Frequency of hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia
  • Exercise habits
  • Sleep quality
  • Stress levels
  • Eating habits
  • Region of residence

2.3 Glucose Data

If you choose to connect your LibreLinkUp/FreeStyle Libre account, we collect:

  • Real-time glucose readings
  • Measurement history
  • Glucose trends

Note: This data is obtained through Abbott’s LibreLinkUp API and requires your explicit authorization.

2.4 User-Generated Content

  • Posts and publications
  • Comments
  • Private messages
  • Shared photos

2.5 Usage Information

  • App interaction data
  • Language preferences
  • App settings

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service: Create profile, connect with other users, publish content, and monitor glucose.
  • Personalization: Customize content and features.
  • Communication: Send important notifications.
  • Security: Protect against fraudulent activities.
  • Improvements: Analyze usage and enhance the app.

4. Information Sharing

4.1 Public Information

The following information may be visible to other users:

  • Name and profile picture
  • Posts marked as public
  • Friends list (when authorized)

4.2 Private Information

We never share without your consent:

  • Detailed health data
  • Private messages
  • Glucose readings
  • Login information

4.3 Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: AWS (Amazon Web Services)
  • Abbott/LibreLinkUp: Only with your authorization
  • Legal authorities: When required by law

5. Storage and Security

5.1 Where We Store It

Your data is stored on secure AWS servers in the USA, using:

  • AWS Cognito
  • AWS AppSync
  • AWS S3

5.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption at rest
  • Secure authentication
  • Role-based access control
  • Continuous monitoring

6. Your Rights

In accordance with the LGPD, you have the right to:

  • Access
  • Correction
  • Deletion
  • Portability
  • Revocation
  • Objection

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary. After account deletion:

  • Profile data: up to 30 days
  • Backups: up to 90 days
  • Anonymized data may be retained

8. Children's Data

TypeD is not intended for children under 13. If we inadvertently collect such data, it will be removed.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes.
